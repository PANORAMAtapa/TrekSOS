Trek Name: Roopkund Mystery Lake Trek
Region: Uttarakhand (Chamoli District, Garhwal)
Max Altitude: 4790 m (15700 ft)
Grade: Moderate to Difficult
Duration: 6 Days
Total Distance: 53 km
Best Season: May to June, September to October
Starting Point: Lohajung
Ending Point: Lohajung

Overview:
Roopkund is legendary for high altitude meadows of Ali and Bedni Bugyal, offering unmatched views of Mt. Trishul and Nanda Ghunti. At the top lies the high-altitude glacial lake shrouded in mystery, surrounded by snow ridges and deep ridges.
