Trek Name: Borasu Pass Trek
Region: Uttarakhand to Himachal Pradesh (Tons Valley to Baspa Valley)
Max Altitude: 5450 m (17880 ft)
Grade: Challenging - High Altitude Pass
Duration: 8 Days
Total Distance: 67 km
Best Season: May to June, September to October
Starting Point: Sankri / Taluka
Ending Point: Chitkul, Kinnaur

Overview:
Borasu Pass is an ancient trade route connecting Har Ki Dun valley in Uttarakhand with the Kinnaur valley of Himachal Pradesh. Trekkers cross dramatic glaciers, moraines, high alpine meadows (bugyals), and cross near the Indo-Tibetan border. Known for high altitude adventure, hanging glaciers, and majestic views of Swargarohini and Bandarpoonch peaks.
