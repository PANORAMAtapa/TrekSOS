Trek Name: Kedarkantha Winter Trek
Region: Uttarakhand (Govind Pashu Vihar National Park)
Max Altitude: 3810 m (12500 ft)
Grade: Easy to Moderate
Duration: 5 Days
Total Distance: 20 km
Best Season: December to April (Snow Summit)
Starting Point: Sankri
Ending Point: Sankri

Overview:
Kedarkantha is India's most popular winter summit trek. Famous for knee-deep snow, campsites surrounded by giant pines, the serene frozen Juda Ka Talab lake, and an exhilarating 360-degree summit sunrise viewing 13 Himalayan peaks including Swargarohini, Black Peak, and Bandarpoonch.
