Trek Name: Hampta Pass Trek
Region: Himachal Pradesh (Kullu to Spiti Valley)
Max Altitude: 4287 m (14065 ft)
Grade: Moderate
Duration: 5 Days
Total Distance: 35 km
Best Season: June to September
Starting Point: Jobra (Manali)
Ending Point: Chatru (Lahaul & Spiti)

Overview:
Hampta Pass is famous for dramatic landscape shifts: transitioning from lush pine forests of Kullu valley to the stark desert landscape of Spiti. Crossing river streams, flower-laden meadows at Jwara and Balu Ka Ghera, culminating in the pass crossing and a drive to the turquoise Chandra Taal lake.
